Hawaiian Incense "ala onaona"

"ala onaona" is incense brand which is handmade in Hawaii. "ala" means "path" and "onaona" means "fragrance" in Hawaiian. The brand is inspired by the natural beauty of Hawaii and the spiritual significance of incense.

Clients: School Projects Project Type: Package Design Competencies: Illustration, Structure, visual communication Software: Illustrator, Photoshop
